Meet MDS · Institutional Mission Nigeria & Africa

Media Diplomacy Studies (MDS) is a specialised research, policy and strategic engagement platform dedicated to advancing the study and practise of media diplomacy, strategic communication, narrative power, perception management, public diplomacy and international image projection. Operating at the intersection of media, diplomacy and international relations, MDS examines how states, institutions and societies communicate identity, build credibility, shape international perceptions and exercise influence in an increasingly complex global information environment. Through research, policy dialogue, publications, high-level convening and scholar-practitioner engagement, MDS seeks to strengthen Nigeria’s and Africa's capacity to communicate credibly, engage strategically and participate more effectively in the global contest for ideas, narratives and influence.

Core Focus & Research Scope

Deepening Understanding of Media & National Power

Media Diplomacy Studies (MDS) is a specialised research, policy and strategic engagement platform envisioned on deepening understanding of the increasingly important relationship among media, diplomacy, strategic communication, international relations and national power. Its work focuses particularly on media diplomacy, public diplomacy, state and international broadcasting, narrative power, credibility, national image, artificial intelligence and emerging communication technologies, examining how these forces shape perceptions, relationships and influence within the contemporary international system.

Media • Diplomacy • International Relations MDS Scope
Action & Scholar-Practitioner Engagement

Moving Beyond Externally Constructed Narratives

MDS brings together scholarship, policy and practice through research, publications, high-level dialogues, institutional partnerships, capacity development and scholar-practitioner engagements. Its central concern is how Nigeria and Africa can move beyond being predominantly subjects of externally constructed narratives towards developing the institutional credibility, intellectual capacity and communication architecture to speak for themselves and engage the world more strategically. Through initiatives including Annual Diplomacy Day, MDS seeks to create sustained platforms for engagement among diplomats, policymakers, scholars, media leaders, strategic communicators, cultural institutions, the private sector and other actors whose work shapes national reputation and international influence.

Research Divisions & Studies Programmes

Research & Studies

Specialised Thematic Research Programmes at the Intersection of Media & Diplomacy

Programme I Research Division

Media Diplomacy & Statecraft

Examines how foreign ministries and diplomatic missions utilize press briefings, multilateral media events, and digital broadcast channels to negotiate bilateral agreements and articulate foreign policy objectives.

Diplomatic Press Envoys Briefings Summit Protocol
Programme II Research Division

Narrative Power & Perception Management

Investigates the mechanisms of international image projection, perception audits, and strategies for overcoming systemic historical biases in global news coverage of Nigeria and the African continent.

Perception Audits Counter-Narratives Reputation Indices
Programme III Research Division

Artificial Intelligence & Emerging Media

Explores the geopolitical implications of algorithmic news delivery, deepfake technology, automated influence campaigns, synthetic media, and generative AI in international diplomacy and security.

AI in Diplomacy Synthetic Media Algorithmic Influence
Programme IV Research Division

State & International Broadcasting

Assesses the strategic reach and modernization of state broadcast institutions (NTA, Voice of Nigeria, FRCN) and compares their international transmission models against global public diplomacy broadcasters.

Broadcast Policy Satellite Diplomacy Soft Power Media
Programme V Research Division

Public Diplomacy & National Image

Studies cultural diplomacy, diaspora communication networks, foreign student exchanges, and strategic trade image projection to enhance sovereign credibility across international capitals.

Nation Branding Cultural Statecraft Diaspora Engagement
Programme VI Research Division

Geopolitics & Information Sovereignty

Investigates information warfare, cyber-diplomacy, cross-border digital surveillance, and policy frameworks necessary for developing nations to defend their digital and informational sovereignty.

Info Sovereignty Cyber Diplomacy Strategic Autonomy
