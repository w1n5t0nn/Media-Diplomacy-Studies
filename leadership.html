Dr. Mkpe Abang

PhD in International Relations & Diplomacy · Member, Nigerian Institute of Public Relations

Dr. Mkpe Abang holds a PhD in International Relations and Diplomacy. Founder of Media Diplomacy Studies, he advances scholarship in state media diplomacy, narrative power and international influence. His intellectual authority draws upon over three decades of distinguished experience in journalism, publishing, strategic communication and public relations practice. He is a member of the Nigerian Institute of Public Relations.
